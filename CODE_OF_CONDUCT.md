# Community conduct

Material Email welcomes careful, constructive participation.

- Be respectful and specific. Critique behavior, designs, and code—not people.
- Protect privacy. Never post someone else’s mail, credentials, logs, screenshots, or identifying data.
- Make accessibility and localization concerns first-class engineering feedback.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, or sustained disruption.
- Accept correction gracefully and leave a clear technical record for the next contributor.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or block accounts when needed to keep the project safe. Report conduct concerns through a private repository-maintainer channel; do not escalate a sensitive report in public.
